      Usage

      Test the microphone

      Say a few words: the bar should move while you speak.

      The most common cause is Windows privacy: Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone > "Let desktop apps access your microphone". Nothing you say during the test is recorded or sent anywhere.

      Turn on talking

      To understand your voice, AI Team Lite needs a speech model (about 145 MB). It is downloaded once and runs on your PC: nothing you say is sent anywhere.

      If Fred does not hear you later: Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone → turn on "Let desktop apps access your microphone".
